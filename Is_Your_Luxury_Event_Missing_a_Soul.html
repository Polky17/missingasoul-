Is Your Luxury Event Missing Soulful Intention and Creative Intrigue?



There are two kinds of high end events. The ones people talk about the next morning with a cheeky smile on their faces and the ones that are already fading by the time the car pulls away. The budget is often similar with an opulent venue, exemplary standard, flowers and the service. All of it completely interchangeable. The difference is whether the experience was built around feeling, or simply built to look as though it was.

If you have ever left a beautiful, glamorous dinner feeling nothing in particular and could not quite explain why, you are not too difficult to impress. You have simply been at a table where soulful intention was not one of the ingredients. You already know, somewhere, that it is the most important one.

This diagnostic measures five dimensions of how you experience and commission luxury events using Sensory Depth, Creative Courage, Guest Connection, The Soul Deficit, and Experience Readiness.

The result will tell you precisely where the opportunity lives and what your events have been missing.

About This Diagnostic 10 questions across 5 dimensions. Takes approximately 4 minutes. Designed for those who create, commission, and attend high-end experiences and feel, somewhere, that something is consistently missing.
